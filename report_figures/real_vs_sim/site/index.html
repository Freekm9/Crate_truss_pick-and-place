Across all runs with video

Planners on the robot's real start and goal joints

Move to pre-grasp: every planner found a path on every repeat of these moves, so success does not separate them here. After post-processing, RRT-Connect, BiTRRT and RRT* reproduce the robot's own plan almost exactly. CHOMP and the joint line follow the same path with different timing, the Cartesian line stays about 1 cm away, and Pink takes a much longer route. These are the moves the robot actually made, from the capture pose to grasps it could reach, so they are easy cases: the benchmark's harder return moves are not part of this set. Without post-processing the sampling planners wander far from the straight path. Five of those 750 plans show a negative wall clearance in the simplified capsule model used for clearance, although MoveIt's own collision check accepted them.

Transport: every planner again found a path on every repeat, and here the path shape does change. The robot moves in a straight Cartesian line at 0.2 speed scaling. The post-processed planners instead take a joint-space path that bows about 6 cm away from that line and is about 2 cm longer, and it takes less time partly because they are retimed at 0.3 speed scaling. The Cartesian line planner stays within 1 cm of the robot's line. These plans leave out the held truss as an obstacle, as the benchmark does, so their clearances do not account for the truss hanging below the gripper.
